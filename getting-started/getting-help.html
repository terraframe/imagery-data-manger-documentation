Getting Help

Support for IDM is provided at two levels. Start with your organization's help page, and contact the IDM help desk only when your organization's support can't resolve the issue.

tip

If an upload or processing job failed, check Troubleshooting Processing Errors first. It explains the error messages IDM shows and how to fix many of them.

1. Your Organization's Help Page​

Each organization can publish its own help page in IDM, with its help desk contacts, support hours, request templates, documentation and training resources. This should be the first place you go for support.

  1. Click Need some help? in the header at the top of any IDM page. The help page opens in a new browser tab.

  2. The page shows the help content for your organization. If your organization doesn't have its own help page, the page for a higher-level organization that it belongs to is shown.

  3. Follow your organization's instructions to request support.

The help page also links to this documentation and to the GeoPlatform documentation.

If the help page is empty or doesn't cover your issue, ask your IDM administrator. Administrators set up the organization help pages; see Help Pages.

2. The IDM Help Desk​

TerraFrame operates a help desk for IDM. Use it for general IDM support when your organization's support isn't able to resolve the issue.

To open a ticket, email support@terraframe.com.

To help the help desk resolve your issue quickly, include:

  • what you were trying to do, and the steps you took
  • any error messages, and screenshots if possible
  • the names of the site, project, mission and collection involved, if any
  • for processing or upload problems, the job's details from the Workflow Tasks page
