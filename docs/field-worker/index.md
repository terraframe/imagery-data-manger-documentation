Field Workers are the primary users of IDM. Their primary tasks are to upload data, process data into products, and create metadata. These guides provide instructions on how to complete different tasks and workflows in the application.

New to IDM? Start with [Getting Started](../getting-started/accessing-idm.md) and the [Data Hierarchy](../getting-started/data-hierarchy.md).

## Typical Workflow

1. **Organize your data.** [Create a site](organizing-data/sites.md) if one doesn't exist, then [create a collection](organizing-data/collections.md) along with any new [projects](organizing-data/projects.md) and [missions](organizing-data/missions.md).
2. **Upload raw data.** Upload [still imagery](uploading-data/still-imagery.md), [multispectral imagery](uploading-data/multispectral-imagery.md), [video](uploading-data/video.md), or [LiDAR](uploading-data/lidar.md) to the collection.
3. **Monitor progress.** Track uploads and processing jobs on the [Workflow Tasks](workflow-tasks.md) page.
4. **Create products.** [Generate products](products/generating-products.md) from your raw data or [upload products](products/uploading-products.md) created with other tools.
5. **Share your data.** [Publish products](publishing-products.md) to the public, share raw images with [image sets](image-sets.md), or [download files](downloading-files.md).
