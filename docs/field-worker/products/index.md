Products are stand-alone assets made up of harmonized imagery, elevation, or LiDAR data. Products in IDM are created from one of two methods.

1. [Derived from raw data](generating-products.md) in a collection using one of the processes in IDM.
2. [Manually uploaded](uploading-products.md) to the products tab of a collection. These are typically created or refined using external tools but uploaded to IDM for storage.

Either way, products are stored in *product groups*. Processing creates a product group named after the *Product name* you enter, and you can create a product group yourself to hold uploaded products. A collection can have several product groups, for example from processing with different settings, and one of them is the primary group. See [Product Groups](generating-products.md#product-groups).

Products can be managed from a collection modal and can be viewed in the Site Viewer.
